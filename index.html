01 — About

About Me

I'm Vaibhav Pawar, Founder of Lord Sai Investment & Share Market Academy and an NJ Wealth Distribution Partner. My work sits at the intersection of financial planning and practical stock market education — helping working professionals, students and business owners understand money instead of fearing it.

My Mission: To make financial planning and stock market knowledge accessible, practical and honest — free of hype, shortcuts and unrealistic promises.

Investment Philosophy: Long-term, goal-based investing built on discipline and financial literacy — not tips, guesswork or gambling.

Why I Started LSI: I saw too many people learning the market from confusing YouTube videos and unreliable tips channels. Lord Sai Investment & Share Market Academy exists to replace that confusion with structured, mentor-led, practical learning.
